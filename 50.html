Arabic-language enthusiast

I am Hasibul Hoque, a Bangladeshi writer, editor and translator interested in Bengali and Arabic language, literature and publishing. My work brings together writing, editorial practice, translation and independent literary initiatives.

As Executive Editor of Al-Midad, an Arabic monthly magazine, I contribute to a project that seeks to encourage Arabic learning and literary engagement in Bangladesh. I am also interested in making meaningful writing accessible to readers and building bridges between languages and traditions.

My ongoing interests include Arabic-to-Bengali and Bengali-to-Arabic translation, Islamic literature, fiction, editorial development and digital platforms for reading and learning.

“Writing today, so that knowledge and ideas may leave a lasting trace.”

Editorial role

Al-Midad Arabic Monthly

A platform for Arabic language, literature and learning in Bangladesh.

المداد
ARABIC MONTHLY MAGAZINE

Executive Editor

Al-Midad is an Arabic monthly magazine working to connect readers with Arabic language and literature. Its editorial vision welcomes learners at different levels and seeks to make Arabic writing, translation, Islamic heritage and literary culture accessible to a wider Bengali readership.

My role connects me with editorial coordination and the development of magazine content. The project reflects my wider commitment to Arabic publishing and literary exchange.
